Follow-up sequence

How should follow-ups behave?


Only used in "until they reply" mode. 7 is the default: roughly one nudge every 40 hours for about 10 days. Most replies come in the first few touches, and it always stops the moment a lead replies or their email bounces.

In "until they reply" mode the fixed steps below are hidden and not used. Follow-ups come from the AI emails instead (steps 1-3), then keep circling back politely until the max is reached, a reply arrives, or the email bounces.

A follow-up sends only if the previous step sent successfully, no reply was detected, and the address did not bounce. The form shows exactly the steps above; each has its own subject and body with the same {variables} and {a|b} spintax. Leave a step's body empty to end the sequence there.
